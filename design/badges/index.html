Badge drafts

Four marks that put the idea — you buy players you cannot fully see — into the badge, beside the current one for comparison. Each is shown at hero size and at the two sizes a phone actually shows an icon.

Current

A man in a suit with a ball. This is you, and it could be any management game ever made.

1 · Redacted

The same man, a bar where his face is. The player you are buying is the one you cannot see.

2 · The range

A scout's range as the crest. Two lit bounds, and somewhere between them a footballer you cannot quite see.

3 · Brackets

[ ● ] — a range with a football in it. The one drawn to be an icon: two strokes and a circle survive 48px.

4 · The stamp

The shield as a press cutting, the word stamped across it the way the fee line reads in every paper. Needs the word, so it is a hero mark, not an icon.
