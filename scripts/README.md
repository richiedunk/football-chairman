# Development scripts

Not part of the build. These exist because a simulation is only as good as what
you can measure about it, and every calibration bug in this project was found by
running one of these rather than by reading code.

| Script | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| `npx tsx scripts/smoke.ts` | Generates a world and prints sample clubs, the best players, top academy prospects, generation time and save size. First thing to run after touching generation. |
| `npx tsx scripts/matchtest.ts` | Simulates 1,200 matches in the top and bottom divisions and reports home/draw/away split, goals, shots and attendance. Guards against the match engine drifting away from real football. |
| `npx tsx scripts/seasontest.ts` | Runs three full seasons and prints finances, board confidence, fan mood, XP and squad size each year. Catches slow drift that a single-week test cannot. |
| `npx tsx scripts/phasetime.ts` | Times each simulation phase separately over a whole world. Use before optimising anything. |
| `npx tsx scripts/cuptest.ts` | Runs a cup to completion and prints the round-by-round field size. |
| `npx tsx scripts/budgetorigin.ts` | Where a club's *opening* wage budget comes from, and whether it agrees with the board routine that sets every budget after it. Prints revenue, upkeep, wage bill, the generated budget and what `recalculateBudgets` would set on the same untouched world, by tier over ten seeds. Found world generation budgeting 58% of a reputation-only proxy that ran to 2.8x real income in non-league, so every club below the top flight opened on money it had never earned. `SEED`, `SIZE`, `SEEDS`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/financecheck.ts` | Revenue, wages, upkeep and net position by division at world creation. Found facility upkeep taking 58% of a non-league club's revenue. |
| `npx tsx scripts/crisischeck.ts` | Runs three careers for a season and reports how much of the world ends up under a transfer embargo. |
| `npx tsx scripts/attrcheck.ts` | Attribute means by division, and how far generated attributes drift from the ability they encode. |
| `npx tsx scripts/staffcheck.ts` | How many unattached staff exist and how many each club can actually hire. |
| `npx tsx scripts/churn.ts` | Every transition players make in and out of professional football, counted per season by diffing the world week to week rather than instrumenting the engine — intake, promotion, free signings, transfers, expiries, retirements — plus where clubs sit against the three squad-size caps. Disproved the theory that `FREE_AGENT_TARGET` was capping squad sizes. |
| `npx tsx scripts/stuckclubs.ts` | For the clubs stuck below the emergency squad floor, how many free agents are affordable and how many clear their ability ceiling. Tests whether a starving club is short of players or is refusing the ones available. |
| `npx tsx scripts/roomcheck.ts` | Whether a leader is worth paying for or the dressing room is just a tax. Swaps one senior player's traits and replays the same season across many seeds. `SEEDS`, `SEASONS`, `SEED`. Comparing league points over three seasons measures nothing — the runs relegate away from each other and score against different opposition. |
| `npx tsx scripts/datacheck.ts` | Whether investment in the data department buys accuracy or only volume — runs the model at every level and checks each finding against the valuation the model cannot see. If level 1 is as accurate as level 20 the investment is decoration and the feature is a lie. |
| `npx tsx scripts/buybackcheck.ts` | Whether buy-back clauses get granted, on whom, and how many come good — a clause nobody grants is dead code and one always worth exercising is a free player. Reports live clauses, how many are worth using against how many are under water, and the ages they land on. |
| `npx tsx scripts/recruitcheck2.ts` | Whether a stated recruitment policy actually changes what a club does — squad size, mean age and foreign share by policy, at world creation and after N seasons. The risk with this feature is that it becomes a label; this is the check that it is not. |
| `npx tsx scripts/continentalcheck.ts` | What each confederation can field, whether every continental competition converges to a winner inside its calendar, the prize ladders, and the extra fixtures a qualified club plays against its own league-mates. `SEASONS`, `SIZE`, `SEED`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/driftcheck.ts` | Where the young drift comes from: the senior squad split by age band each season, and the three flows across the line that matters — under-21s having a birthday into the 21+ pool, 21+ players signed in, and 21+ players leaving. A pool shrinks because fewer arrive or more leave; this says which, after three explanations were ruled out by measurement. `SIZE`, `SEASONS`, `SEED`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/academycheck.ts` | What actually happens to an academy player — the share who ever play a senior game, how long they sit before promotion, and whether the free-agent pool is released academy boys or professionals with careers behind them. The academy is the largest single flow in the game, so the fix for it is completely different depending on which. `SIZE`, `SEASONS`, `SEED`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/setpiececheck.ts` | Whether the set-piece coach is an edge or an inflation. Plays the same fixtures in one world with and without the coaches, because adding a staff member shifts every random draw downstream and comparing totals across builds measures the seed rather than the change. `SEED`, `MATCHES`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/worldhash.ts` | A sha256 of the whole world after a seeded run, plus the tick speed. An optimisation that changes no behaviour must reproduce the hash exactly — tests assert ranges and invariants, so a change that shifted every player by one would pass most of them. `SEED`, `WEEKS`, `SIZE`. Strict on purpose: a real behaviour change moves it too, which forces "did I mean that?" to be asked out loud. |
| `npx tsx scripts/driftcheck.ts` | The senior squad split into age bands, and the three flows across the line that matters: under-21s having a birthday into the 21-and-over pool, arrivals from outside the professional game, and departures from it. A pool shrinks because fewer arrive or more leave, and this says which. `SIZE`, `SEASONS`, `SEED`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/academycheck.ts` | What actually becomes of an academy player — promoted, played, released, deleted — and who is really in the free-agent pool. Settled that the pool is two-thirds released professionals rather than academy washouts, which killed a proposed yearly cull before it was built. `SIZE`, `SEASONS`, `SEED`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/setpiececheck.ts` | Whether the set-piece coach is an edge or an inflation: the same fixtures played in one world with and without the coaches. A division where everybody employs one must not be a higher-scoring division. `SEED`, `MATCHES`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/namedcheck.ts` | Which limit actually stops a club naming a squad — the 25-place list, the 17 non-homegrown places, or simply not having enough players aged 21+. Counts it club by club, mid-season. Settled that registration was never the constraint and that the falling registered count is squads getting younger. `SIZE`, `SEASONS`, `SEED`. |
| `npx tsx scripts/intlcheck.ts` | What an international break actually costs: how many players a club loses per break by division and standing, how many are at international standard at all, and how many carry a tournament premium afterwards. `SEED`, `SEASONS`. A break nobody notices is a feature that does not exist; one that guts the side is a punishment. |
| `npx tsx scripts/savegrowth.ts` | Runs a full career and reports the save size — raw JSON and gzipped, which is what IndexedDB actually holds — every five seasons, with a per-key breakdown. `SIZE=compact|standard|large`. Answers how big a save gets and what is making it bigger. |
| `npx tsx scripts/population.ts` | Player population by bucket — senior, academy, free agent — season by season. `SIZE`, `SEASONS`, `SEED`. Found the free-agent pool growing eightfold while contracted seniors fell by 1,700. |
| `npx tsx scripts/linkcheck.ts` | Plays three seasons, collects every link an inbox message or news story carried, and checks each one against the real route table — both as written and again against the world as it stands, since an inbox item outlives the player it names. Found nothing after the media fix; the point is that it stays at nothing. |
| `node scripts/namefit.mjs` | Walks every screen that lists names in the built app at 390x844 and measures the real name box against the real font. Set `LIST_NAME_BUDGET` from this, not by eye — the guessed 22 was abbreviating 23-character names in a 288px box. Needs the preview server. |
| `node scripts/e2e.mjs` | Drives the built app in a real mobile-sized browser and screenshots every screen. Needs `npm run build && npx vite preview` first, and `SHOT=<dir>` for the screenshots. |
